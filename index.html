    About Me

    Hi — I'm Ben Minahan. I've been making music in one form or another my whole life: I play guitar, bass and piano, and I sing. More recently I've taken up drums and banjo. I love making beats, arranging melodies, and creating unusual sounds in my home studio in Kansas City, MO. My goal is to write songs people enjoy listening to while having fun doing it.

    Over the years I've had the chance to play a wide range of music with some extremely talented musicians, but I'm always working on solo material as well. Since 2020 I've released 16 original songs across a variety of genres.

    In 2025 I'm planning to play more live shows and can't wait to see you there. Until then, I invite you to listen to my music for free (on YouTube and here). If you like what you hear, please share it with others — or buy a physical CD if you're feeling nostalgic.

    Cheers,
    Ben
