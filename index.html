    About

    Music shaped by years of performance, writing, and collaboration.

    Hi — I'm Ben Minahan, and music has been part of my life since grade school. I sing, play bass, guitar, piano, and I’ve also picked up drums and banjo over the years.

    My experience spans choir, all-state honors, bands across many genres, home recordings, live shows, and acoustic performances at coffee shops and open mic nights. I’ve spent years building a sound that feels open, honest, and human.

    In 2026 I’m planning to play more live shows and can’t wait to share that energy with you. Until then, feel free to listen, share, and enjoy the music for free.

    Background

    • All-state choir and barbershop quartet experience
    • Multiple bands across rock, metal, punk, and electronic styles
    • Home studio recording and original songwriting
